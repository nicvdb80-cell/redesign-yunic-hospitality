Ubud

The setting

Ubud and the surrounding villages — Tegalalang, Payangan, Mas, Penestanan — are where some of Indonesia's oldest culinary traditions still live. The morning markets here are different from the coast. More roots. More spice. More ceremony.

The experience

What we bring to your villa

Your villa in Ubud has something a restaurant never can — the sound of the rice fields, the open air, the feeling of being somewhere completely private. We cook in that space. We serve in that space. The whole evening belongs to you.

Questions

Before you book

Do you travel to all areas of Ubud?

Yes. We serve any villa or private residence in Ubud and the surrounding area. Just share your address when you get in touch and we will confirm.

How much notice do you need?

We recommend at least 48 hours. For weekends and peak season, earlier is better. If you are on short notice, contact us anyway — we will do our best.

What do I need to provide?

Share your location and kitchen details. We confirm the facilities, equipment, tableware and any rentals needed before booking.

Can you accommodate dietary requirements?

Tell us about dietary requirements and allergies before booking. We will confirm what we can accommodate for your menu and setting.
