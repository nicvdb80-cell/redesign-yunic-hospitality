Days at sea deserve evenings worth surfacing for.

Great diving deserves great food. We create culinary experiences for boutique dive resorts, liveaboards and yachts: sharing tables, à la carte menus, set menus and romantic dinners for two after a day beneath the surface. Through menu development, pop-ups and collaborations, we bring Yunic to your destination. We share our recipes and techniques with your chefs, so they can continue bringing a taste of Yunic to your guests long after our visit.

The right fit matters.

We don’t believe the same concept works everywhere.

Before we say yes, we want to understand the place, the people behind it and what you want to create. If we feel Yunic can bring something meaningful to it, we take it from there.

That could be a pop-up, a collaboration, sharing dishes, an à la carte menu, set menus, romantic dinners for two or sharing recipes and techniques with your chefs. We agree a scope that suits your destination, facilities and guests.

There is no fixed formula. It starts with a conversation and seeing where it can go.
