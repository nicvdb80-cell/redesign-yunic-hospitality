Days at sea deserve
evenings worth surfacing for.

Dive & Dine is built around a simple truth: the best diving days deserve food that matches them. We create sharing and à la carte menus for dive resorts, liveaboards and yachts, sometimes launched with a Yunic pop-up or collaboration, and train your kitchen team to cook them every day, long after we leave.

Who we
work with.

We work with dive resorts, liveaboards and private yachts where food is part of the experience, not simply something served between dives.

What we do depends on the place. We look at what is available locally, how the kitchen works, who the guests are and what makes sense for the destination.

Dive resorts

A Yunic pop-up or collaboration at your resort, leaving behind a sharing or à la carte menu your team keeps cooking for your guests, every service.

Liveaboards

Menus created for life at sea and for your galley, so your crew can cook them trip after trip.

Private charters & yachts

A menu shaped around the boat, the route and the guests, handed over to your chef to keep using.

For divers

Dive with your group at a resort or on a boat with a taste of Yunic on the menu. Ask us where.

The right fit
matters.

We don’t believe the same concept works everywhere.

Before we say yes, we want to understand the place, the people behind it and what you want to create. If we feel Yunic can bring something meaningful to it, we take it from there.

That could be a pop-up or collaboration, a sharing menu, a full à la carte menu or training for your kitchen team, built to keep working long after we leave.

There is no fixed formula. It starts with a conversation and seeing where it can go.
