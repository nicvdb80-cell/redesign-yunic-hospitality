Around the table.

Our Story

Yuli, Nic and the idea behind Yunic.

Yunic grew from Yuli and Nic’s shared life in Bali and a wish to create something of their own. It brings together her Indonesian warmth, his Belgian roots and the pleasure they both find in gathering people around good food.

Yuli, founder and owner, looks after the hosting, organisation and details that make guests feel welcome. Nic leads the culinary direction. His Indonesian journey began at CasCades in 2013. He went on to become the culinary founder of Apéritif in Ubud: an idea that became reality, and a restaurant whose reputation now reaches well beyond Indonesia. Apéritif remains close to his heart, and it will keep growing stronger. Yunic gives Nic and Yuli the freedom to create something entirely their own, and that freedom is something he is grateful for every day.

With Yunic, they have room to explore together, inspired by their travels, their love of the ocean and the people they meet along the way.

Something of their world, brought into yours.
