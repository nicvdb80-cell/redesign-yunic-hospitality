03 — About Yunic

Two backgrounds. A shared way of looking at food and hospitality.

Yunic began with Yuli and Nic.

Yuli grew up in Indonesia, where food is part of everyday life and sharing a table is how people come together. She brings her Indonesian roots, a deep understanding of flavour and a natural instinct for hospitality: making people feel welcome and looked after.

Nic brings his European culinary background, classical technique and a chef’s way of looking at ingredients. Years of cooking and travelling around the world, a constant source of inspiration, and more than a decade of living in Indonesia continue to shape how he cooks.

They learned from each other.

That exchange became the foundation of Yunic: European technique, Indonesian flavour and everything they have picked up along the way. Sometimes the menu is European, sometimes Indonesian, sometimes something in between. It always starts with great ingredients and the people at the table.

But Yunic isn’t only about food.

Both are certified divers. Nic has spent more than 26 years diving around the world and eventually brought Yuli into that world too. Travel, diving, markets, restaurants, people and places continue to shape how they think about food and hospitality.

What connects it all is simple:

They want people to have a great experience.

A private chef dinner at home. A Yunic Rijsttafel. A canapé party by the pool. A company evening. A dive resort with a taste of Yunic on the menu.

Whatever the occasion, Yuli, Nic and the Yunic team shape it around the people at the table.

And that is why Yunic doesn’t accept every inquiry.

There needs to be a connection between what you are looking for and what Yunic can genuinely bring to it. If there isn’t, we’d rather say so and, when we can, introduce you to someone who may be a better fit.

Perhaps not for everyone.
Maybe for you.
